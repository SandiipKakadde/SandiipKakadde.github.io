About me

I write documentation that engineers trust and end users can follow. For the past decade, I've sat between product teams and their audiences at companies like Persistent Systems, SLB (Schlumberger), and Emerson — turning complex systems into structured, standards-compliant content.

My current focus is AI-assisted documentation: designing prompt frameworks to accelerate content generation while keeping quality high, and reviewing AI-generated output for technical accuracy, tone, and voice. Before that, I spent five years at Schlumberger writing equipment manuals and training writers as well as engineers on plain language, Simplified Technical English (STE) and DITA.

The work spans DITA-based product help, feature documentation, SOPs, and integration guides. What stays constant is the goal: make information findable, accurate, and useful under real-world conditions.
